## 📚 Reading List  *(the lab bookshelf)*

> *Curated starter list — mix of classics and rabbit holes. Not all finished — that's the point.*

### ⚛️ Physics
- **Feynman Lectures on Physics** (Feynman, Leighton, Sands) — the gold standard, vol. I–III
- **Classical Mechanics** (Goldstein) — Lagrangian & Hamiltonian formalism
- **Introduction to Electrodynamics** (Griffiths) — EM from first principles
- **Principles of Quantum Mechanics** (Shankar) — rigorous but readable
- **The Elegant Universe** (Brian Greene) — strings & extra dimensions (pop-sci but fun)

### 🧪 Chemistry
- **Physical Chemistry** (Atkins & de Paula) — thermodynamics, kinetics, quantum chem
- **Organic Chemistry** (Clayden et al.) — the definitive organic reference
- **The Disappearing Spoon** (Sam Kean) — periodic table storytelling (great bathroom read)
- **Molecular Quantum Mechanics** (Atkins & Friedman) — bridges physics & chemistry

### ⚙️ Systems & Design
- **Thinking in Systems** (Donella Meadows) — feedback, stocks, flows — mind-bending
- **The Art of Systems Architecting** (Maier & Rechtin) — heuristics for complex systems
- **Code** (Charles Petzold) — how computers work from first principles
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** (Kleppmann) — distributed systems done right
- **The Systems Bible** (Gall) — short, funny, disturbingly accurate
- **A Pattern Language** (Alexander et al.) — design patterns beyond software

### 📝 Papers / Resources
- [The Feynman Technique](https://fs.blog/feynman-learning-technique/) — learning by teaching
- [Paul Halmos — How to Write Mathematics (PDF)](https://www.mathematicsgenerator.com/Halmos_How_to_Write_Mathematics.pdf)
- [Nand2Tetris](https://www.nand2tetris.org/) — build a computer from logic gates up
- [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/) — puzzle-driven systems/algorithm practice
